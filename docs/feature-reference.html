?How the Help Guide Is Easy

MyPaiOS documents itself inside the app — no separate wiki, no outdated docs site. The whole guide is one page, reachable from anywhere with /help, and it's built to be used, not just read:

  • Instant search. Press / anywhere to jump into a search box that filters all 39 sections live as you type — no page reload, no "search results" page to wait on.
  • Everything collapsed except what you need. The sidebar groups all 39 sections into 8 topics (Getting Started, Chat & Pi, Research & Knowledge, Agents & Automation, Media & Gallery, Email & Calendar, Skills & Settings, Privacy & Security). Only the group you're reading expands — you're never scrolling past 30 irrelevant headings to find one answer.
  • An interface map up front. Before any deep-dive section, one page shows exactly where every tool lives on screen, so a brand-new user gets oriented in under a minute, not by trial and error.
  • Guided tours that teach by doing. The bigger tools (Library, Cookbook, Deep Research, Compare, Theme, Settings, Gallery) auto-play a short walkthrough the first time you open them — pointing at the exact button, not describing it in prose — then never repeat unless you ask with a slash command.
  • Keyboard-first throughout. Ctrl+K for quick search, Ctrl+B to toggle the sidebar, documented right where you'd need them, not buried in a separate shortcuts page.
  • Take it with you. One click exports the entire guide to a real PDF — every section expanded, properly paginated — for offline reading or handing to a teammate.

The result: the guide answers "how do I…" in the time it takes to press one key and type two words, not by hunting through a table of contents.
