Electron — https://github.com/electron/electron
Declared as a devDependency in desktop-electron/package.json (electron ^33.2.0,
electron-builder ^25.1.8). No Electron binaries are committed to this
repository — npm installs them on your machine.

IMPORTANT, for packaged desktop builds only: `npm run dist:mac` /
`dist:win` / the AppImage target produce artifacts that DO redistribute
third-party binaries — the Electron framework, which embeds Chromium
(BSD-3-Clause plus several hundred further third-party licences), Node.js (MIT)
and V8 (BSD-3-Clause). Electron ships `LICENSE` and `LICENSES.chromium.html`
for exactly this reason, and electron-builder copies both into the app bundle
by default. Any published .dmg / .exe / .AppImage must retain them; verify
their presence in desktop-electron/dist/ before releasing.

electron-builder is likewise MIT (https://github.com/electron-userland/electron-builder).
