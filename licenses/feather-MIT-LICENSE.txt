Feather Icons — https://github.com/feathericons/feather
Used as: inline SVG icon markup throughout static/index.html, static/js/**,
static/login.html, static/style.css, docs/index.html, src/visual_report.py,
MyPaiOS-Help-Guide.html and desktop-electron/loading.html.

Feather was never installed as a dependency — its icon paths were pasted
inline. Some icons are used verbatim; others are hand-modified variants or
recombinations of Feather paths, which remain derivative works of Feather and
are covered by the notice below.

Verified: 79 distinct Feather icons (feather-icons@4.29.2) appear in this
repository with every path/polyline/polygon primitive byte-identical to
upstream, across roughly 660 occurrences.
