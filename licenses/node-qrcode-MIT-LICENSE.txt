Provenance caveat (version not assertable)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------
static/lib/qrcode.min.js (24,853 bytes,
sha256 0935de514006b84fe54404b4a98fb3b5cc45478d9efa64d75e19719025663c19)
is an esbuild IIFE bundle of node-qrcode's browser entry point. It matches NO
published node-qrcode artifact: 1.4.x ships a browserify build of a different
shape and size, 1.5.0-1.5.1 ship an unminified build/qrcode.js, and 1.5.2+
ship no browser bundle at all. It IS node-qrcode (its exact internals are
present: getSymbolSize, getSymbolTotalCodewords, getBCHDigit, toSJIS, the
version-capacity table, the L/M/Q/H bit map, BitBuffer, BitMatrix), but it was
built outside the project and its upstream version cannot be asserted. It
arrived with the upstream Odysseus fork and should be re-vendored from a
pinned release.
